Why I Created This Site

TheTycoon Tools was born out of a desire to simplify the complex world of business management in The Tycoon. As the server grew and more businesses were added, tracking stock, money, and rotation timers became a daunting task.

I wanted to create a central hub where players could easily import their data, monitor their progress, and optimize their gameplay without needing external spreadsheets or manual calculations. This site is my way of giving back to the community and making the simulation experience more enjoyable for everyone.
